Design analogies, rather than claims of copied mechanics: *Papers, Please* suggests that legible administrative choices can carry a story; *Mini Motorways* suggests a small readable map can create meaningful spatial decisions; management games such as *Two Point Hospital* suggest that capacity and queues can turn income into planning; arcade pursuit games suggest that handling and recovery matter more here than detailed vehicle simulation. These are creative reference points from general knowledge, not external source evidence or instructions to reproduce their assets.

The recommended sequence is an inference from scope: prove aiming and placement before investing in a full campaign, exploit the shared world for both hands-on and management play, and use local seeded results before a hosted leaderboard. Human playtests decide whether those choices succeed.
